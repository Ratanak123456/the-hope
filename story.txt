The important twist is:

The scientists believe they discovered an ancient Mecha trapped in the ice. They don’t realize the Mecha is actually the lock keeping an alien imprisoned beneath it.

Chapter One: The Frozen Signal

Decades before the invasion, satellites occasionally detected an unusual energy signal beneath the North Pole.

The signal would appear for several seconds and then disappear for years. No machine could identify its source. It did not match any known natural energy, military technology, or human communication system.

Most scientists believed it was an equipment error.

But Dr. Elian Voss did not.

After studying the signal for most of his career, Voss discovered that it repeated according to a deliberate pattern. It was not a distress call.

It was a warning.

Then, after years of silence, the signal returned.

This time, it did not disappear.

The North Pole Expedition

A secret research organization sent a team of scientists, engineers, soldiers, and excavation workers to the North Pole.

Their mission was officially described as a climate research expedition. In reality, they were searching for the source of the signal.

Dr. Voss led the research team. Kai’s mother, Dr. Lyra Ren, was the expedition’s specialist in ancient languages and unknown technology.

For weeks, the team traveled across the frozen landscape without finding anything. Their equipment frequently failed. Compasses pointed in different directions, radios produced strange voices, and several workers claimed to feel something moving beneath the ice.

Eventually, the signal led them to an enormous iceberg surrounded by a permanent storm.

The iceberg was different from the ice around it. Its surface was almost black, and perfectly straight lines could be seen deep inside it.

The expedition established a research base around the iceberg and began drilling.

The deeper they went, the warmer the ice became.

That should have been impossible.

After several days, the drilling equipment struck metal.

The team melted an entrance through the ice and discovered an enormous underground structure. It was not a crashed aircraft or a human military base.

It was a tomb.

The Ancient Mecha

Inside the frozen chamber, the scientists discovered the upper body of a gigantic humanoid machine.

It was kneeling beneath the ice with both arms extended toward the ground. Its armor was made from an unknown ivory-colored material. Dark mechanical structures were visible between its armor plates, while faded golden lines covered its body.

The machine appeared ancient, but its body showed signs of battle:

One shoulder had been torn open.
Several blades were buried in its back.
Half of its faceplate was damaged.
Its hands were locked around enormous chains leading deeper beneath the ice.

Voss believed they had discovered the greatest weapon in human history.

Lyra disagreed.

“This machine wasn’t buried here,” she said.

She examined its position and the chains extending from its hands.

“It chose to remain here.”

The scientists named it Aegis Zero, believing it might be the first and strongest of an unknown class of Mecha.

The soldiers wanted to remove it immediately. If they could understand its technology, humanity would possess a weapon more powerful than anything ever created.

But Lyra found something else.

Behind the Mecha was a wall covered in ancient symbols.

The symbols were not instructions for activating it.

They were a warning:

When the Guardian rises, the Prisoner rises with it.

The Guardian is not sleeping.

The Guardian is holding the door closed.

What Was Beneath the Mecha

The team followed the chains beneath Aegis Zero.

Deep below the first chamber, they discovered a second creature frozen inside the ice.

At first, it appeared to be another machine.

Then its body moved.

It was much larger than Aegis Zero. It possessed a long armored body, six folded limbs and a head covered in closed violet eyes. Black material had spread outward from its body like frozen roots.

This was not one of the ordinary Wardens that would later attack Earth.

It was their ruler.

Its original name had been lost, but the ancient inscription called it:

The Sovereign Below.

Thousands of years earlier, the Sovereign had arrived on Earth while hunting the last surviving Aegis Frame. Aegis Zero defeated it, but the battle damaged the Mecha’s core.

Aegis Zero no longer possessed enough power to destroy the creature.

Instead, it used its own body as the final piece of a planetary seal.

The Mecha had remained conscious beneath the ice for thousands of years, using every remaining part of its energy to keep the Sovereign imprisoned.

The strange signal detected by the scientists was not Aegis Zero asking to be rescued.

It was warning humanity to stay away.

The Forbidden Message

The soldiers refused to abandon the discovery.

They ordered the research team to activate the Mecha.

Lyra protested. She showed them the translated warning, but the expedition commander believed she was afraid of losing control of the discovery.

The team found a circular mechanism inside Aegis Zero’s chest. Above it was a short sequence of ancient symbols.

Unlike the warning on the wall, these symbols appeared to be an activation message.

Lyra translated them:

To the one who inherits our final light:

Speak your will, and the Guardian shall rise.

The expedition commander ordered her to read it aloud.

Lyra refused.

He threatened to remove her from the project and use an incomplete machine translation instead. She realized that an incorrect activation could be even more dangerous.

Believing she might still be able to control the awakening, Lyra placed her hand against the mechanism.

She read the ancient message.

“Guardian of the final light, awaken and answer humanity’s call.”

For the first time in thousands of years, orange light appeared inside Aegis Zero’s chest.

Its fingers moved.

The entire frozen chamber began shaking.

The scientists celebrated because they believed the activation had succeeded.

Then the chains broke.

The Alien Awakens

As Aegis Zero lifted its head, the seal beneath it opened.

Cracks spread across the ice surrounding the Sovereign.

One violet eye opened.

Then another.

Then hundreds of smaller lights appeared beneath the ice.

The scientists had not discovered only one alien.

An entire Warden army had been sealed beneath the North Pole with it.

The excavation alarms activated. Soldiers aimed their weapons toward the lower chamber as black mechanical creatures began climbing through the broken ice.

Aegis Zero attempted to stand, but its core was too weak. It had spent nearly all its energy maintaining the seal.

The Sovereign spoke through every radio inside the facility:

“The Guardian lives.”

The researchers heard the next message in their own minds.

“Then the harvest may begin again.”

The Warden army attacked the expedition.

Lyra’s Final Decision

Lyra realized they could no longer restore the original seal.

Their only chance was to separate Aegis Zero’s core from its body before the Sovereign captured it.

She entered the Mecha’s damaged chamber while Voss attempted to evacuate the surviving researchers.

Inside Aegis Zero, Lyra discovered that the machine was alive.

It showed her memories of ancient worlds destroyed by the alien empire. She saw other Aegis Frames scattered across the galaxy. She also learned that the Sovereign was not the true ruler of the aliens.

It was only the commander of the army buried on Earth.

If it contacted its empire, a much larger force would come.

Aegis Zero asked Lyra to become its pilot, but she was not fully compatible. The connection would kill her.

However, the machine detected another compatible person through her memories.

Her young son.

Kai.

Lyra removed a small fragment of Aegis Zero’s core and placed it inside the metal wrist device she had brought with her. The fragment contained the Mecha’s consciousness, its power and the key required to awaken it again.

She gave the device to Voss.

“Take this to my son.”

Voss stared at her.

“What are you going to do?”

Lyra looked toward the creatures entering the chamber.

“Give him enough time to grow up.”

She activated an emergency energy surge inside the facility.

The explosion collapsed the iceberg, burying Aegis Zero and the Warden army beneath millions of tons of ice once again.

Voss escaped with only a few survivors.

Lyra did not return.

The Hidden Mistake

The world was told that the expedition had been destroyed by an earthquake.

Voss returned to Nova City and gave the wrist device to Kai’s uncle. He did not explain its true purpose because he feared the government—or the aliens—would discover Kai.

For years, the North Pole remained silent.

But the second seal was weaker than the first.

The excavation had awakened the Sovereign, and it could now send faint messages beyond Earth.

Slowly, the signal traveled through space.

Years later, something answered.

A massive alien carrier changed direction and began traveling toward Earth.

At the same time, the core fragment around Kai’s wrist activated.

Aegis Zero had finally found its pilot.

And beneath the North Pole, the Sovereign opened all of its eyes.

Transition to the Player

The North Pole prologue ends with the research facility collapsing around Lyra.

The screen becomes completely dark.

For several seconds, the player hears only Lyra’s breathing.

Then she records one final message:

“Kai, if you ever hear this, I’m sorry. We thought we discovered humanity’s greatest weapon. We didn’t understand that it was protecting us.”

“The machine will call for you one day. When it does, you must decide what kind of person will inherit its power.”

“And Kai… whatever speaks from beneath the ice, do not believe it.”

A loud impact ends the recording.

Silence.

Then an alarm clock begins ringing.

The darkness fades into a peaceful morning in Nova City.

Kai wakes up years later, unaware that this is the final normal day of his life.

A faint orange light pulses from the device beside his bed.

Opening Cinematic: The Guardian Beneath the Ice
Opening Shot

The screen begins completely black.

A weak radio transmission can be heard through static.

“Arctic Expedition Seven to Command. We have reached the signal’s origin.”

The static becomes louder.

“There is something beneath the ice.”

A deep metallic pulse interrupts the transmission.

The screen slowly fades in.

An enormous snowstorm covers the North Pole. Strong winds move across an endless frozen landscape. Through the storm, several research vehicles travel toward a dark iceberg rising above the surrounding ice.

The vehicles appear tiny beside it.

A title appears:

THE NORTH POLE
FIFTEEN YEARS BEFORE THE INVASION

The camera passes over a temporary research base constructed around the iceberg. Scientists operate scanning equipment while soldiers guard the excavation site. Large drilling machines face the ice.

Red warning lights flash through the snowstorm.

The Signal

Inside the command tent, a group of scientists studies a three-dimensional scan of the iceberg.

At its center is the outline of something enormous and humanoid.

A repeating signal appears on their screens:

Three slow pulses. A pause. Three slow pulses.

Dr. Elian Voss studies the pattern.

Beside him stands Dr. Lyra Ren, a specialist in ancient languages and unknown technology.

The expedition commander, General Corvin Hale, enters the tent.

GENERAL HALE: “Report.”

Voss enlarges the scan.

DR. VOSS: “The signal is approximately two kilometers beneath the surface. Whatever is producing it is larger than any machine we have ever constructed.”

GENERAL HALE: “A spacecraft?”

DR. VOSS: “Possibly.”

Lyra examines the repeating signal.

LYRA: “It isn’t calling us.”

Hale turns toward her.

GENERAL HALE: “Then what is it doing?”

Lyra listens as the three pulses repeat.

LYRA: “I don’t know yet.”

Another pulse travels through the base.

The lights flicker.

For a fraction of a second, something that sounds like a distant roar can be heard through every radio.

The scientists become silent.

Hale looks toward the excavation monitors.

GENERAL HALE: “Begin drilling.”

Breaking Through the Ice

A montage shows the expedition digging deeper into the iceberg.

Powerful drills cut through thick layers of ice. Workers place explosives along a newly discovered wall. Scientists examine fragments containing an unknown black material.

As the excavation continues, strange events begin happening around the base:

Compasses rotate without stopping.
Radios receive whispers on unused frequencies.
Electronic clocks lose several minutes.
Workers discover enormous claw-like marks inside the ice.
The temperature increases as the team moves deeper.
Several soldiers report seeing violet lights beneath the frozen ground.

One worker places his hand against the ice.

Something moves on the other side.

Before he can react, the movement disappears.

The team eventually reaches a perfectly smooth wall beneath the natural ice.

It is made from an ivory-colored metal that shows no rust or damage.

Ancient symbols cover its surface.

Lyra touches one of the symbols.

A line of orange light travels across the wall.

The entire structure begins opening.

Warm air escapes from the darkness beyond it.

The expedition members stare into the newly revealed passage.

They expected a frozen cave.

Instead, they discovered a doorway.

The Frozen Chamber

The scientists and soldiers enter the structure.

Their lights reveal enormous pillars disappearing into darkness. Human-made equipment looks small against the ancient architecture.

The walls contain carvings of giant machines fighting creatures descending from the sky.

Some carvings show cities being destroyed.

Others show planets covered in black roots.

At the end of the passage, the team reaches a massive chamber.

Their lights move across two gigantic metal feet.

The camera slowly travels upward.

A humanoid machine is kneeling inside the ice.

Its ivory armor is cracked and damaged. Dark mechanical structures are visible between its armor plates. Faded golden lines cover its chest and arms.

Several massive weapons are buried in its back.

Its head is lowered.

Both hands are pressed against a circular structure beneath it.

The entire expedition becomes silent.

DR. VOSS: “My God.”

General Hale steps closer.

GENERAL HALE: “How old is it?”

Voss scans the surrounding material.

DR. VOSS: “The ice around this chamber is thousands of years old.”

Hale looks up at the machine.

GENERAL HALE: “Then we have just discovered the oldest weapon in human history.”

Lyra does not answer.

She is looking at the Mecha’s hands.

Huge black chains are wrapped around its arms. They continue downward through the circular structure beneath it.

The Mecha is not simply kneeling.

It is holding something down.

The Warning

The team establishes equipment inside the chamber.

Scientists scan the Mecha while soldiers photograph its armor and weapons. Engineers connect cables to a circular mechanism inside its chest.

The expedition names the machine Aegis Zero.

General Hale orders the engineers to prepare it for removal.

Lyra continues translating the symbols around the chamber.

She finds a large inscription hidden beneath layers of ice behind the Mecha.

Voss joins her.

DR. VOSS: “Can you read it?”

LYRA: “Some of it.”

She clears more ice from the wall.

Images appear between the symbols:

A monstrous creature descending toward Earth.
Aegis Zero fighting it.
The creature lying beneath the ground.
The Mecha kneeling above it.
Chains connecting them.
A final image showing both figures awakening together.

Lyra’s expression changes.

LYRA: “We need to stop the excavation.”

DR. VOSS: “Why?”

She reads the first translated line.

LYRA: “The Guardian is not buried.”

She reveals the next line.

LYRA: “The Guardian is the seal.”

A deep impact comes from beneath the floor.

Dust falls from the ceiling.

The scientists stop working.

Another impact follows.

This one is stronger.

Somewhere below them, an enormous chain tightens.

Something Else in the Ice

Lyra, Voss and a small group of soldiers follow the chains through a lower passage.

The walls become darker as they descend. The orange markings disappear and are replaced by black organic material frozen inside the structure.

Their lights begin flickering.

They reach an observation platform overlooking a deeper cavern.

At first, they can see nothing but ice.

Then lightning from the expedition equipment illuminates a shape inside it.

A giant alien creature is frozen beneath Aegis Zero.

It is larger than the Mecha.

Its body is covered in black armor. Six long limbs are folded around its torso. Sharp structures extend from its back like broken wings.

Its head faces upward toward the Mecha.

Hundreds of smaller creatures surround it inside the ice.

An entire army is buried beneath the chamber.

One soldier whispers:

SOLDIER: “The machine wasn’t protecting itself from them.”

Lyra stares at the frozen alien.

LYRA: “It was protecting us.”

One of the creature’s fingers moves.

Everyone steps backward.

Voss checks his scanner.

DR. VOSS: “No biological activity. It could have been movement in the ice.”

Lyra looks at him.

LYRA: “The ice is moving because it is waking up.”

The Activation Order

Lyra and Voss return to the main chamber and demand that the excavation stop.

General Hale refuses.

The expedition has already connected an external power system to Aegis Zero’s chest. Engineers have discovered a sequence of symbols around its core.

GENERAL HALE: “This machine could change human history.”

LYRA: “It is already holding back the thing that tried to end it.”

GENERAL HALE: “Or it defeated that creature and trapped itself in the process.”

LYRA: “You don’t understand what we found.”

Hale looks at the ancient machine.

GENERAL HALE: “That is why we need to wake it.”

Lyra stands between Hale and the activation system.

LYRA: “If the Mecha rises, the seal opens.”

Hale orders the soldiers to move her.

Voss attempts to disable the power system, but the engineers have already completed the connection.

A mechanical translation program analyzes the activation inscription.

An artificial voice begins reading the ancient words aloud.

Lyra struggles against the soldiers.

LYRA: “Stop the translation!”

The first symbol lights up.

Orange energy travels across Aegis Zero’s chest.

LYRA: “It isn’t an activation code!”

A second symbol activates.

The chains around the Mecha begin shaking.

GENERAL HALE: “Then what is it?”

Lyra looks at the full inscription.

She finally understands its meaning.

LYRA: “It is asking for a new guardian.”

The final symbol activates.

Aegis Zero Awakens

All lights inside the expedition base turn off.

The chamber falls into complete darkness.

The researchers can only hear the wind moving through the tunnels.

Then a single orange light appears inside Aegis Zero’s chest.

The light expands through the engraved lines covering its body.

Its fingers move against the seal.

Ice breaks away from its arms.

Mechanical parts inside its neck begin rotating.

Aegis Zero slowly raises its head.

The researchers stare upward in silence.

The Mecha’s eyes open.

Golden light fills the chamber.

Several scientists begin celebrating.

General Hale looks at the machine with amazement.

GENERAL HALE: “We did it.”

Lyra looks down.

The seal beneath Aegis Zero is opening.

LYRA: “No.”

The chains begin rising from the ground.

One breaks.

Then another.

Aegis Zero attempts to push itself back down, but the human power system continues forcing energy into its core.

The machine is not trying to stand.

It is trying to remain kneeling.

LYRA: “Turn off the power! It’s fighting the activation!”

The engineers attempt to disconnect the system.

The controls no longer respond.

Aegis Zero releases a distorted mechanical sound. It resembles a cry of pain.

The final chain breaks.

The Prisoner Awakens

Far beneath the chamber, a crack forms across the frozen alien’s face.

One violet eye opens.

Then a second.

Then six more.

Every radio in the expedition activates simultaneously.

Static fills the chamber.

A voice begins speaking through the equipment, but it is not using any human language.

The sound enters the minds of everyone inside the facility.

UNKNOWN VOICE: “The Guardian rises.”

The ice surrounding the smaller creatures begins cracking.

Black hands press against the frozen walls.

UNKNOWN VOICE: “The gate is open.”

The giant creature unfolds one of its arms.

The entire iceberg shakes.

UNKNOWN VOICE: “The harvest may continue.”

Warning alarms activate throughout the base.

Scientists run toward the exits. Soldiers aim their weapons at the lower tunnels. Emergency lights cover the chamber in red.

The first smaller alien breaks through the ice.

It crawls into the light, unfolds its armored body and releases an unnatural scream.

More violet eyes open behind it.

General Hale finally understands what they have done.

GENERAL HALE: “Seal the chamber!”

Lyra looks at the broken chains surrounding Aegis Zero.

LYRA: “There is no seal anymore.”

The aliens attack.
